Data sources

  • Live camera feed: SEAHIVECAM (Wahoo Bay) primary, City of Pompano Beach pier cam fallback, recorded clips for visual continuity overnight.
  • Fish detections: YOLOv26-nano + DINOv2/ViT classifier (Fishial.ai, MIT). Currently the stock 866-class model — fine-tuning on Wahoo Bay-specific species is pending the volunteer-annotated dataset.
  • Water quality: SenseStream wahoo_2 deployment (EXO2 sonde). Source-tagged live when an auth token is configured, synthetic while one is pending from the SenseStream contact.
  • Weather: SenseStream co-located weather station + supplementary forecast layer. Same source-tagging as the sonde.
  • Reviewer corrections: opened via the correct button on Recent events; written to detection_corrections with reviewer email, confidence, and notes.

Refresh & ownership

  • Last refreshed: —
  • Live cadence: raw stream at the camera's native fps (~15 fps when light); inferred frames at the rate the GPU sustains.

Metric definitions

  • Fish Sightings: count of distinct persistent tracks (not count of per-frame bbox events) — a fish lingering in front of the camera is one sighting.
  • Species Found: distinct species IDs across sightings in the window.
  • Species Richness: the same count, scoped to a specific time bucket or site.
  • Water Clarity: qualitative banding of turbidity_fnu (lower is clearer); thresholds are indicative until reviewed by the marine-biology stakeholders.
  • Inference fps: rate at which the detector + classifier process frames (typically < live grab rate when the scene is busy).

Known limitations

  • The classifier was trained on a globally-mixed fish dataset; confident mislabels on Atlantic-reef species are routine and the most valuable thing reviewers can correct.
  • Fallback periods (camera dark, autoswitch on) emit no new sightings or sensor writes — gaps in the time series are intentional, not missing-data bugs.
  • Sensor data is currently synthetic stub readings (deterministic per-day seed); live data flips on as soon as the SenseStream token is provided.
  • Habitat dimension (reef vs sand vs wreck) is not yet attached to detections; habitat-by-species views are deferred until habitat is hand-labelled per camera/zone.

This dashboard is built for education, conservation, and community access. Treat values as a learning & exploration tool; review assumptions before any public-release decisions.
